# The bunker principles

bunker is a place for friends to talk without anyone watching, measuring or selling. These are the rules every choice is
held against.

1. **End-to-end encrypted.** Closed chats, photos, video and voice are locked on the phone with a code only the friends have.
   The server sees noise.
2. **No accounts.** A phone is a phone. No email, no phone number, no real name. A pseudonym is a choice.
3. **No logs.** No access log, no IP addresses on disk, no statistics, no ads, no trackers, no third parties.
4. **No back-ups.** What is deleted is gone, for everyone including the owner.
5. **Everything expires.** A chat keeps a handful of messages, a board its newest threads, and a chat nobody uses disappears.
   The red messages show what goes next. You can always delete your own.
6. **Anonymous by default.** You are `Anon #XXXX`. A name is something you claim, and nobody can take it over.
7. **Honest.** Boards and open chats are public and say so. The admin can delete, not read. A website cannot prove its owner
   did not change the code: that is why the code is open and you can run your own.
8. **Small and calm.** Text and ASCII. No algorithm, no endless scroll, no scores. A `<3` is only a `<3`.
9. **The owner is the weak spot, on purpose.** As little access as possible, as little stored as possible.
10. **Friends first.** It is built for people who know each other. It does not have to grow.

*Defend your right to free and secure internet communications.*
